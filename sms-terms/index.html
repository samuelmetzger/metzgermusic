Program name

Samuel Metzger Music Texts

Program description

Samuel Metzger (MetzgerMusic), a sole-proprietor organist and church musician in Nashville, TN, sends text messages to musicians, colleagues, and church members who opt in. Messages coordinate church music: service scheduling, rehearsal times, and service details. There is no marketing or promotional content.

How to opt in

You can opt in by entering your mobile number and checking the optional, unchecked SMS consent box on the contact form, or by giving Samuel your number in person or by email and replying yes when he asks permission to text you. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. After you opt in you receive: "Samuel Metzger Music Texts: You're subscribed to church music scheduling texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. To rejoin, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or email samuel@metzgermusic.com.

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your mobile number and opt-in information will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy.
